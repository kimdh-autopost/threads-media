Privacy Policy

SavingTips Poster is a personal publishing tool operated by an individual creator to schedule and publish content to the creator's own Threads and Instagram accounts (@saving_tips.kr, @jachui.salim, @korean.loves.india).

What data we access

With the account owner's permission, the app accesses the owner's own profile information, media, post insights and comments on the owner's own posts, solely to publish scheduled posts and to measure their performance.

What we do not do

We do not access accounts of other users, do not sell or share any data with third parties, do not use data for advertising, and do not send messages to anyone.

Storage

Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets. Post performance numbers are stored privately for the owner's own analysis. Comment text from public replies is used only to understand what the audience wants to see next and is never republished with usernames.

Data deletion

See the data deletion instructions. You may also email us and we will delete any data within 30 days.
